This 漢字 deals with \textit{life} in all its guises.  Along with the ideas of \textit{birth}, \textit{production}, and \textit{growing}, it also incorporates the notions of \textit{pure}, \textit{raw}, and \textit{fresh}, in everything from beer to silk.\\\hline
When used as a suffix, it will often indicate \textit{student}.
\end{glyph}
